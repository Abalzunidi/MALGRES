Privacy Policy

PDPL Compliant – Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

This Privacy Policy is issued in compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its implementing regulations. It explains how MALGRÉ collects, processes, stores, and protects Personal Data.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge and agree to this Policy.

1. Controller Information

Data Controller: MALGRÉ

MALGRÉ is the entity responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing Personal Data in accordance with the PDPL.

2. Definitions

  • Personal Data: Any data that identifies or can identify an individual, directly or indirectly.
  • Processing: Any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
  • Data Subject: The individual whose Personal Data is processed.

3. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of Personal Data:

a) Identification & Contact Data

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and billing address

b) Transactional Data

  • Order details and purchase history
  • Delivery and return information

c) Technical Data

  • IP address
  • Device type, browser information
  • Website usage and interaction data

d) Payment Data

Payment information is processed securely by licensed third-party payment providers. MALGRÉ does not store card details.

4. Purpose of Processing

Personal Data is processed for legitimate and specific purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling orders and providing products and services
  • Managing payments, shipping, returns, and exchanges
  • Providing customer support and communications
  • Improving our website and services
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, and PDPL obligations

5. Legal Basis for Processing (PDPL)

We process Personal Data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract with the Data Subject
  • Legal obligation under Saudi laws
  • Legitimate interests of the Company without prejudice to Data Subject rights
  • Explicit consent, where required under the PDPL

6. Consent

Where processing is based on consent:

  • Consent is obtained explicitly and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Personal Data may be disclosed only to:

  • Authorized service providers (shipping companies, payment gateways, IT providers)
  • Government authorities where legally required

All third parties are contractually obligated to comply with PDPL requirements and data protection standards.

8. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Personal Data may be transferred or processed outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia only in accordance with PDPL requirements, ensuring:

  • Adequate level of data protection
  • Regulatory approval where required

9. Data Retention

  • Personal Data is retained only for the period necessary to achieve the stated purposes or to meet legal obligations.
  • Data is securely deleted or anonymized once no longer required.

10. Data Security

MALGRÉ applies appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data from:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Loss, misuse, or unlawful processing

Security measures include encryption, access controls, and secure hosting environments.

11. Data Subject Rights (PDPL)

Under the PDPL, you have the right to:

  • Access your Personal Data
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate data
  • Request destruction of Personal Data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Be informed of the legal basis and purpose of processing

Requests may be submitted through our official communication channels.

12. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

You may disable cookies through browser settings; however, this may affect site functionality.

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly process Personal Data of minors.
