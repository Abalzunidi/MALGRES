Return Policy

MALGRÉ

This Return & Exchange Policy is established in accordance with applicable e-commerce laws and regulations and governs the contractual relationship between MALGRÉ ("the Company") and its customers.

Article (1): Definitions

  • Company: MALGRÉ, the owner and operator of the MALGRÉ brand.
  • Customer: Any individual or legal entity purchasing products through the Company's official sales channels.
  • Product(s): Perfumes and fragrance-related items sold by MALGRÉ.
  • Contract: The agreement formed upon completion of a purchase order.

Article (2): Scope of Application

This Policy applies to all purchases made through:

  • The official MALGRÉ website
  • Any authorized online sales platforms operated by the Company

This Policy does not apply to purchases made through unauthorized resellers or third parties.

Article (3): Right of Return and Exchange

  1. The Customer has the right to request a return or exchange within 7 calendar days from the date of receipt, in accordance with applicable e-commerce regulations.
  2. To be eligible, the Product must:
    • Be unused, unopened, and untested
    • Be in its original condition and packaging
    • Include all accessories, labels, and proof of purchase

Article (4): Non-Returnable Items

For health, hygiene, and quality control reasons, returns or exchanges will not be accepted in the following cases:

  • Products that have been opened, used, or tampered with
  • Consumable goods that cannot be returned for sanitary reasons
  • Discounted items, promotional offers, or final sale items
  • Complimentary samples or gifts
  • Requests submitted after the legal return period

Article (5): Damaged or Incorrect Products

  1. If a Product is received damaged, leaked, or incorrect, the Customer must notify the Company within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Clear photos or videos evidencing the issue must be provided.
  3. Upon verification, MALGRÉ shall, at its discretion:
    • Replace the Product at no additional cost, or
    • Refund the full amount paid, including shipping fees

Article (6): Return Procedure

  1. The Customer must submit a formal return or exchange request via the Company's official communication channels.
  2. Prior approval from MALGRÉ is required before returning any Product.
  3. The Product must be shipped according to the provided instructions.
  4. Any return sent without prior authorization may be rejected.

Article (7): Refunds

  • Returned Products will be inspected upon receipt.
  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5–10 business days.
  • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.
  • Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to Company error.

Article (8): Exchanges

  • Exchanges are subject to product availability.
  • Any price difference will be settled accordingly with the Customer.

Article (9): Order Cancellation

  • Orders may be canceled free of charge before shipment.
  • Once shipped, the order becomes subject to this Return & Exchange Policy.

Article (10): Limitation of Liability

  • MALGRÉ is not responsible for dissatisfaction due to personal scent preferences.
  • The Company is not liable for delays or damages caused by third-party shipping providers.
  • The Company bears no responsibility for improper storage or misuse after delivery.

Article (11): Force Majeure

MALGRÉ shall not be held liable for failure or delay in performance due to events beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters or governmental actions.

Article (12): Amendments

MALGRÉ reserves the right to amend or update this Policy at any time. Any changes shall become effective upon publication on the official website.

Article (13): Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and the competent courts therein shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes.
